The menus inside the game in Brazilian Portuguese: the VR menu and the Trainer. 🇧🇷

## ⬇️ Download

Download **`GTAVXR-PTBR-Setup-v1.1.0.exe`** below, close GTAVXR and run it. It installs only for your Windows user and does not ask for an administrator password. Installing it over 1.0.0 is fine.

The installer is not digitally signed yet, so Windows may show a warning before it runs. Choose **More info** and then **Run anyway**.

Step by step, with pictures: [Download and install](https://github.com/scarletmodder/gtavxr-ptbr#%EF%B8%8F-download-and-install) (English) or [Baixar e instalar](https://github.com/scarletmodder/gtavxr-ptbr/blob/main/README.pt-BR.md#%EF%B8%8F-baixar-e-instalar) (Português).

## ✅ What you need

- A PC with Windows 10 or 11
- [GTAVXR](https://github.com/LVL-VR/GTAVXR/releases) 1.0.2, made by LVL-VR, downloaded from its official page

No file of GTAVXR comes with this installer or with the in-game mod.

## 🌎 What is new

- **The VR menu inside the game in Portuguese**: 308 of 308 texts.
- **The Trainer inside the game in Portuguese**: 776 of 776 texts.
- They come in a small GTAVXR mod of our own, **GTAVXR PT-BR menus do jogo**, which the installer adds to your GTAVXR library and turns on in your profiles (option **Also translate the menus inside the game**, checked). It changes the texts in memory while you play, so no GTAVXR file is changed and GTAVXR's check of its game files still passes.
- In the game: VR menu, **Settings**, switch **Language** until the texts are in Portuguese. Portuguese takes the place of Spanish.
- The mod is also attached on its own, **`GTAVXR-PTBR-InGame-v1.1.0.zip`**, for players who prefer to add it in the Suite themselves.
- Uninstalling also takes the mod out of the library and the profiles.

## ⚠️ Known limitations

- The in-game mod was checked against copies of the real GTAVXR 1.0.2 files and accepted by GTAVXR's mod import, but a session in the headset with it has not been recorded yet.
- Inside the game, the language option may still say Spanish.
- A profile created after the install starts without the mod: turn **GTAVXR PT-BR menus do jogo** on in the Suite.
- After updating GTAVXR, run this installer again.
