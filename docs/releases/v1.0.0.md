The first version: the GTAVXR Suite in Brazilian Portuguese. 🇧🇷

## ⬇️ Download

Download **`GTAVXR-PTBR-Setup-v1.0.0.exe`** below, close GTAVXR and run it. It installs only for your Windows user and does not ask for an administrator password.

The installer is not digitally signed yet, so Windows may show a warning before it runs. Choose **More info** and then **Run anyway**.

Step by step, with pictures: [Download and install](https://github.com/scarletmodder/gtavxr-ptbr#%EF%B8%8F-download-and-install) (English) or [Baixar e instalar](https://github.com/scarletmodder/gtavxr-ptbr/blob/main/README.pt-BR.md#%EF%B8%8F-baixar-e-instalar) (Português).

## ✅ What you need

- A PC with Windows 10 or 11
- [GTAVXR](https://github.com/LVL-VR/GTAVXR/releases) 1.0.2, made by LVL-VR, downloaded from its official page

No file of GTAVXR comes with this installer. It translates the copy you already have and keeps the original next to it; uninstalling puts the original back.

## 🌎 What is in it

- The whole Suite in Portuguese: 422 of 422 texts of GTAVXR 1.0.2 (Play, Mods and Settings pages, messages, profile editor, explanations).
- Names and descriptions of the content mods: 129 of 129 texts.
- The language button reads **Português** and **English**.
- An installer in English and Portuguese that finds GTAVXR, checks the file before changing it, and opens the official GTAVXR page when it is missing.
- A clean uninstall: the original `GTAVXR.exe` and the previous language come back.

## ⚠️ Known limitations

- The VR menu and the Trainer inside the game stay in English or Spanish. GTAVXR checks its game files before every launch, so they cannot be translated from outside; their translation (1,084 texts) was offered to the GTAVXR authors.
- After updating GTAVXR, run this installer again.
- The first Play after translating may rebuild the content mods once.
- Tested on Windows 11 with GTAVXR 1.0.2, without a full VR session recorded.
